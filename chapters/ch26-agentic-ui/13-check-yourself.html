Why does streaming make the agent feel better to use?

Reveal ▸

It shows the answer and the steps as they are produced, so you watch the agent work instead of staring at a blank screen. The wait is the same, but it feels far shorter and you can spot a wrong turn early.

What is generative UI, and how is it different from plain text?

Reveal ▸

Instead of returning only text, the agent picks a ready-made interface piece, a chart, form, or map, and fills it with the answer. It chooses how to present the content, not just what to say, usually from a vetted component library for safety.

Why use tiered approval instead of asking about everything?

Reveal ▸

Asking about every action causes alert fatigue: people approve blindly and the gate stops protecting them. Tiers auto-approve safe, reversible actions and reserve the blocking prompt for costly or irreversible ones, keeping oversight where it counts.
