26.8 Accessibility and trust

Trust is earned by being transparent and recoverable

Trust is not a button you add; it grows when a system behaves predictably, explains itself, and lets mistakes be undone. Compare two ways an agent could report booking a flight. Both are correct, but only one builds confidence.

Low-trust reply

"I have booked your flight. Confirmation: AA1234."

High-trust reply

  • The search parameters it used
  • The alternatives it considered and why it chose this one
  • The exact action taken, with a link to the booking
  • An undo option valid for 24 hours
  • A note on what it cannot do: change the booking
Think of it like a contractor who not only says "it's fixed" but shows you the work, the receipts, and how to undo it if you change your mind. The extra detail is exactly what makes you call them again.

In plain terms

Undo and rollbackEvery consequential action should be reversible where the technology allows it.
Audit trailA timestamped log of every action and approval, exportable for review.
