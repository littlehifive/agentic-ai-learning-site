26.3.4 Approval gates · human in the loop

Pause and ask before anything costly or irreversible

An approval gate stops the agent at a risky step and hands the decision to a person. The trick is balance: enough context to choose well, but not so many prompts that people start clicking approve without reading. A good gate states the action, flags the risk, and offers approve, modify, or reject.
