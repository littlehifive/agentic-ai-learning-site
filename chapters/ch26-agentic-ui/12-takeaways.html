Key takeaways

Three things to carry forward

1

Show the process

A chat bubble shows a result; an agent interface shows the work. Streaming, tool cards, and context panels are the foundation of trust, not extras.

2

Keep a human in control

Pause before anything costly or irreversible. Tier the prompts by risk so safe actions flow and only the dangerous ones stop for a yes.

3

Make mistakes cheap

Undo, rollback, and audit trails matter as much as raw capability. The north star is a transparent collaborator whose actions are always recoverable.
