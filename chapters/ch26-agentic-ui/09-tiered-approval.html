26.7 Human-in-the-loop UI design

Not everything needs a pause: sort actions by risk

A flat policy fails both ways: approve everything and people stop reading; approve nothing and the agent is useless. A tiered policy auto-approves safe, reversible actions and saves the blocking prompts for the ones that can really cost you.

Tier 1

Auto-approve

Low-risk, reversible, routine. The agent just proceeds, but logs it for the record.

Examples: web search, reading a file, read-only lookups.

Tier 2

Notify

Medium risk. A non-blocking note appears with a short window to cancel before it finalizes.

Examples: saving a draft email, posting to a sandbox.

Tier 3

Require approval

High-risk, irreversible, or costly. The agent stops and waits for an explicit yes.

Examples: sending mail, deleting data, making a purchase.

In plain terms

Tiered policyMatch the amount of friction to the stakes of the action.
Configurable thresholdsUsers set rules ("always ask before sending email") or the agent learns them over time.
