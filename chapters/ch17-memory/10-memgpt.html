17.3.5 MemGPT and virtual context management

Page memory in and out, like an operating system

MemGPT treats the limited context like a computer's fast but small memory, and external storage like its large but slow disk. The agent itself moves items between tiers, pulling in what it needs and evicting what it does not.

Hot tier

In context

What is loaded right now. Instant to use, but tiny. The most relevant and important items are paged in here.

Warm tier

Fast retrieval store

A searchable index just outside the window. Quick to query and brought into the hot tier on demand.

Cold tier

Archival storage

Effectively unlimited, slow to scan, kept for the long tail and for auditing. Drilled into only when needed.

Think of it like virtual memory on a laptop. The system keeps the pages you are using in fast RAM and swaps the rest out to disk, so a small workspace can serve a huge body of information. The agent decides what to swap based on recency, relevance, and importance.
