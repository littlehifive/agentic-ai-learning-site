17.1 The context-window bottleneck

Three ways an agent fails without memory

1

It forgets the context

Once an event scrolls out of the window it is lost for good. The agent cannot point back to a decision it made earlier in the same task.

2

It cannot learn from experience

With no record of past attempts, every episode is the first. Strategies that worked are not reused, and the same mistakes get repeated.

3

It cannot personalize

Preferences, facts, and history must be re-established every session. The user keeps re-explaining themselves, which is slow and frustrating.

Think of it like meeting a brilliant colleague who has total amnesia each morning. Talented in the moment, but you re-brief them from zero every single day.
