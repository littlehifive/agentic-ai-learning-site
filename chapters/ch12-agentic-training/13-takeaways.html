Key takeaways

Three things to carry forward

1

Grade the whole job

Agentic training rewards an entire multi-step trajectory, not one token. Like a chess game, you judge by the final result, then work out which moves earned it.

2

Credit is the hard part

With a sparse reward at the end, the real challenge is finding which of many steps deserved the blame or the praise, using checkpoints and trajectory slicing.

3

Execution is the best teacher

Self-improvement methods like STaR, Reflexion, Voyager, and LATS all converge on the same anchor: rewards from real execution that cannot be faked.
