How agents learned to improve themselves

Each method added what the last one was missing

2022

STaR

Learns from its own wins. Keeps the attempts it got right and trains on them, so reasoning improves with no human grader. But it only learns from successes it stumbles into.

→cannot learn from failures

2023

Reflexion

Learns from failures too. Writes a verbal critique after a miss and retries with that lesson in the prompt, no weight update. But the notes vanish once the task is over.

→memory is not kept

2023

Voyager

Keeps what it learns. Stores verified skills in a lasting library and composes them for new tasks, so progress accumulates. But it still commits to one path at a time.

→no look-ahead

2023

LATS

Looks before it leaps. Searches several candidate paths and plays them forward before choosing, trading extra compute for markedly better trajectories.

In plain terms

The throughlineEach step widens what the agent can learn from: its wins, then its losses, then a lasting memory, then options it has not tried yet.
Not a strict ladderThese are complementary tools, often combined, rather than one replacing the next.
