Why reward a whole trajectory instead of each step?

Reveal ▸

Because success is only clear at the end. Like a chess game, you cannot reliably score each move as it happens; you play to the finish, see if the task succeeded, and use that single verdict to grade the whole sequence of decisions.

What is the credit-assignment problem?

Reveal ▸

When twenty steps end in failure, a single zero punishes all of them, even the good ones. Credit assignment is the work of finding which steps actually caused the outcome, using checkpoint rewards along the way or tracing back to the first step that broke things.

How do STaR and Reflexion differ?

Reveal ▸

STaR changes the model: it keeps its own correct attempts and fine-tunes on them. Reflexion changes only the prompt: it writes a plain-language critique after a failure and pastes that lesson into the next try, with no weight update at all.
