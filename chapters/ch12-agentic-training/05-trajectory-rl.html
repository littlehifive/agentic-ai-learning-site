12.8 State-of-the-art RL for LLM agents

Reward the whole episode, not a single token

Standard alignment grades one response: good prompt in, good answer out. Agentic RL grades the entire trajectory at once. The model plays out a full attempt, the environment says whether the task succeeded, and that single verdict is the reward used to nudge the whole sequence of decisions.

Think of it like judging a chess game by the final result. You do not score each move as it happens. You play to the end, see who won, and only then work backward to figure out which moves deserve the credit.

In plain terms

Trajectory-level rewardOne score for the entire multi-step attempt, given at the end.
Verifiable rewardA reward that comes from real execution, like a test suite passing, so it cannot be faked or argued with.
GRPOThe common recipe: run several full attempts at the same task, then reward each one relative to the group's average.
