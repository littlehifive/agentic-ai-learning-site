12.2 Trajectory buffers for LLM agents

One full attempt at a task is called a trajectory

A trajectory is the whole episode: every state the agent saw, every action it took, and what it observed in return. Here is a real one, fixing a failing test. Notice the reward stays at zero until the very last step.

1 Think: "Let me read the file first." → reads utils.py 0
2 Think: "The bug is an off-by-one on line 42." → edits the file 0
3 Think: "Let me verify the fix." → runs the tests, all pass +1.0
Think of it like a flight recorder. It logs every reading and every control input across the whole flight, so afterwards you can study the entire run, not just the landing.

In plain terms

TrajectoryThe full sequence of state, action, and observation for one attempt at a task.
Trajectory bufferA store of past attempts, kept as text and tool output, that training can sample from.
Sparse rewardA score that arrives only at the end, so most steps get a flat zero along the way.
