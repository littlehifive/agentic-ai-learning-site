4.3 Stage 2: reward model training

A reward model turns "I prefer this one" into a number

You cannot ask a person to grade every answer the model will ever produce. So you collect a smaller set of this is better than that comparisons, and train a model to predict them. That model can then score any new answer, even ones no human saw.
