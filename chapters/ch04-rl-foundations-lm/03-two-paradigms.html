4.1 Two paradigms for RL in LLMs

Two reasons to use RL, one shared machine

RL is applied to language models for two different goals. The difference is simply where the score comes from: a human judgment, or an objective check.

Paradigm 1

Alignment from human preferences

Make the model helpful, harmless, and honest. People compare answers, and the model learns to write the kind people prefer. This is RLHF, and its shortcut DPO.

Paradigm 2

Capability from verifiable rewards

Teach new skills like math, code, and reasoning. The score comes from a checker: is the answer correct, do the tests pass. This is RLVR, the engine behind modern reasoning models.

Think of it like two coaches with the same playbook. One coaches taste and manners by watching which answer people like better; the other coaches accuracy by checking the answer against a key. Same training method, different judge.
