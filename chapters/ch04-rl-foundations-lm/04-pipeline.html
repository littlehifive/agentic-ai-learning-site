4.3 The RLHF pipeline

Four stages: demonstrate, judge, optimize, repeat

Stage 1

Fine-tune on demonstrations

Start from a pretrained model and teach it to follow instructions by copying good example answers. This gives a base policy to improve.

→

Stage 2

Train a reward model

Show people pairs of answers and ask which is better. A reward model learns to predict those preferences as a single score.

→

Stage 3

Optimize the policy

Let the model generate answers, score them with the reward model, and nudge its weights toward higher-scoring writing, kept close to the start.

→

Stage 4

Evaluate and iterate

Test the aligned model, gather the cases where it still fails, and feed them back into the next round of training.

A KL penalty keeps Stage 3 tethered to the Stage 1 model so it improves without drifting

In plain terms

PolicyThe model being trained: at each step it decides what to write next.
Reward modelA stand-in judge that turns "which answer is better" into a number the trainer can chase.
For RLVRStages 1 and 2 change: train on reasoning traces, and swap the reward model for a checker. Stage 3 is the same.
