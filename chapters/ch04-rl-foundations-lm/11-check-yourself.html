Why do we need RL when fine-tuning already exists?

Reveal ▸

Fine-tuning only imitates its example answers, so it can never beat the quality of that data. RL lets the model generate its own answers, get scored, and improve past the demonstrations.

What does the KL penalty do, and why bother?

Reveal ▸

It is a leash that keeps the trained model close to the original. Without it, the model chases the reward by gaming the judge and drifts into nonsense. The leash keeps improvement honest.

When would you pick RLVR over RLHF?

Reveal ▸

When a machine can check the answer: math, code, logic. A verifier grades each attempt for free, so you skip the human judge entirely. For taste, tone, or safety you still need human preferences.
