11.2.4 Pipeline Parallelism

Hand the model down an assembly line of GPUs

A model is a tall stack of layers. Pipeline parallelism gives the first few layers to one GPU, the next few to another, and so on. Data flows down the line, each GPU doing its stretch of the work before passing it along.

GPU 1

Layers 1 to 20

Takes the input and does the first stretch of processing.

→

GPU 2

Layers 21 to 40

Picks up where GPU 1 left off and carries on.

→

GPU 3

Layers 41 to 60

Continues the middle of the stack.

→

GPU 4

Layers 61 to 80

Finishes the work and produces the output.

Think of it like a car assembly line: each station bolts on its part and slides the car to the next. The danger is idle time. If only one car is on the line, three stations stand waiting, so you keep many cars moving at once to keep everyone busy.

In plain terms

Pipeline parallelismSplitting layers into stages on different GPUs, passing data down the line.
Tensor vs pipelineTensor splits one layer sideways across GPUs; pipeline splits the stack of layers into stages along the line.
The bubbleIdle GPUs waiting their turn. Feeding many small batches at once keeps the line full.
