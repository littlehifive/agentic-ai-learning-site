Why can a 70B model not just train on one GPU?

Reveal ▸

Feedback training keeps four models in memory plus the optimizer's high-precision backup copies and gradients. That totals around 1500 GB, roughly twenty GPUs' worth, while one GPU holds only 80 GB.

What is the difference between tensor and pipeline parallelism?

Reveal ▸

Tensor parallelism splits one wide layer sideways across GPUs that work on it together. Pipeline parallelism splits the tall stack of layers into stages, like an assembly line, with each GPU owning a stretch.

Why split generation and training onto separate clusters?

Reveal ▸

Writing answers is limited by memory speed; learning is limited by raw compute. No single setup is best for both, so each phase gets its own tuned cluster, and they overlap to save time.
