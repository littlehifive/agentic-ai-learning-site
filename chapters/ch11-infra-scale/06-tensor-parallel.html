11.2.2 Tensor Parallelism

Split one layer across several GPUs

Some single layers are so wide that even one of them is a strain. Tensor parallelism cuts an individual layer into vertical strips, hands each strip to a different GPU, and has them compute their part at the same time, then combine the answer.

Think of it like four cashiers ringing up one giant shopping cart together, each taking a section of the items, then adding their subtotals at the end. The catch: they have to talk to each other constantly, so they must sit side by side, not in different buildings.
