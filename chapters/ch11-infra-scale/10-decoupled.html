11.4 Decoupled Architecture

Put the writing and the learning on separate clusters

Since writing answers and learning need different hardware, production systems stop forcing them onto the same machines. They run a fast writing cluster, tuned for memory, and a separate training cluster, tuned for compute, with a buffer in between.

Step 1

Generate

A writing cluster, often running vLLM, churns out practice answers fast.

→

Step 2

Score

The reward model rates each answer for quality.

→

Step 3

Buffer

Scored answers collect in a shared holding area, ready to be used.

→

Step 4

Train

A training cluster updates the model, then ships the new weights back to the writers.

Think of it like a kitchen with a separate prep station and cooking line. While the line cooks one order (training), the prep station is already working on the next (generating), so neither sits idle waiting for the other.

In plain terms

DecoupledSeparating the fast writing phase from the heavy training phase so each runs on its best hardware.
vLLMA popular serving engine that makes the writing phase much faster and is the usual choice for the generation cluster.
OverlapWriting the next round while training on the last gives a sizeable speedup, roughly a third faster.
