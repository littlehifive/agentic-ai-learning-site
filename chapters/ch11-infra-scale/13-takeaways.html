Key takeaways

Three things to carry forward

1

The job outgrows one GPU

Four models plus the optimizer's backups add up to many times a single GPU's memory. Everything else is a way to spread that load across many GPUs.

2

Different splits, different squeezes

Sharding saves memory, tensor parallelism handles a too-wide layer, pipeline parallelism handles a too-tall stack. Reach for the lightest that makes it fit.

3

Separate, overlap, and survive

Writing answers and learning need different hardware, so split them onto separate clusters, overlap them for speed, and checkpoint often because failures are certain.
