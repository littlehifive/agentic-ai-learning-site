11.7 Fault Tolerance at Scale

With hundreds of GPUs, something always breaks

Any single GPU is reliable, but run hundreds for days and failures are a certainty, not a risk. A multi-day run will hit several. Without a plan, one dead chip wipes out everyone's progress, so the system is built to expect and survive failures.

5 to 15 failures expected during a single multi-day training run on a large cluster. The fix is to save progress often and recover automatically rather than start over.
Think of it like writing a long document: you do not retype from scratch when the power flickers because you saved a few minutes ago. Checkpoints are those autosaves, and the system reloads the last one and carries on with one fewer machine.

In plain terms

CheckpointA saved snapshot of the full training state, taken every so often in the background so it does not stall the run.
Fault toleranceDetecting a failed machine, dropping it, and resuming from the last checkpoint without human help.
Elastic trainingCarrying on with fewer GPUs while a replacement spins up, instead of halting.
