11.1 The 4-Model Memory Challenge

The whole job is roughly twenty GPUs of memory

Add up everything a 70-billion-parameter model needs to train with feedback: the weights, the backup copies the optimizer keeps, the gradients, and the helper models. The pile dwarfs the model alone.

Policy weights140 GB
Optimizer backup copies840 GB
Gradients140 GB
Reference model140 GB
Reward model140 GB
Activations and generation cache70 GB
Total to hold at once~1500 GB

One GPU holds 80 GB

One GPU
The job

Divide 1500 GB by 80 GB and you need about 19 to 20 GPUs minimum, before any of the overhead of coordinating them. One machine simply cannot do it.

Think of it like fitting a shipping container of furniture into the trunk of one car. No matter how you pack it, you need a fleet of vehicles.
