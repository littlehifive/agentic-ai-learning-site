20.3.6 General assistant tasks

GAIA: general questions that need tools and reasoning

GAIA asks the kind of question a capable assistant should handle but no single fact lookup can answer. Getting it right means chaining several tools together, a web search, then some code, then reading a file, across a few steps of reasoning. Questions come in three difficulty levels, sorted by how many steps they take.

Think of it like a research scavenger hunt. No one clue gives the answer; you have to search, calculate, open a document, and stitch the pieces together. Easy for a determined person, still hard for an agent.
