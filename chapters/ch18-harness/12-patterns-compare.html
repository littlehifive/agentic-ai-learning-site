18.5 Orchestration patterns

Two ways to decide what to do next

Orchestration is how the agent chooses its next move. There is no single best pattern. The right one depends on whether the task is exploratory and unpredictable, or structured and known in advance.

ReAct loop

  • Decides one step at a time, reacting to each result
  • Adapts well to surprises and dead ends
  • Uses more model calls, since it thinks at every step
  • Best for exploratory tasks with an unknown path

Plan-and-execute

  • Writes the full plan first, then runs it in order
  • Cheaper and faster on long, predictable tasks
  • Slower to react when a step surprises it
  • Best for structured tasks with clear steps
Think of it like exploring versus following a recipe. In an unfamiliar kitchen you taste and adjust as you go; with a recipe you have made before you just run the steps. For the hardest jobs, several specialist agents can be combined under one supervisor.
