18.5.4 Human-in-the-loop

Know when to stop and ask

A good agent does not barrel ahead on every action. Before anything risky or anything it is unsure about, the harness pauses and brings a person in. The model still does the work; the human approves the moments that matter.

Approval gates

Before an action that cannot be undone, such as sending an email or deleting a file, require an explicit yes from a person.

Escalation rules

Hand off when confidence is low, when the task falls outside the agent's scope, or when a safety rule is tripped.

Feedback that sticks

A human correction goes back into the context and can update the plan, so the fix carries forward.

Think of it like a junior employee with a company card. They handle small purchases on their own, but anything large, unusual, or irreversible needs a manager's sign-off first.

Approval gateA checkpoint where a person must confirm before an irreversible action runs.
