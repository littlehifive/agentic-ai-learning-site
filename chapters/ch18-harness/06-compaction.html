18.2.3 Context compression

When the window fills, compact the old turns

As a conversation grows, the history outgrows its share of the budget. Rather than let the oldest text fall off a cliff, the harness shrinks it on purpose while keeping what still matters.

Summarize old turns

Replace the earliest part of the chat with a short summary, often five to ten times shorter, written by a smaller and cheaper model. Recent turns stay word for word.

Keep what is relevant

Score each message by how related it is to the current question, plus how recent it is. Keep the top scorers, and pin the system prompt and the original task so they never drop.

Think of it like meeting minutes. You do not keep a word-for-word transcript of every past meeting. You keep recent notes in full and boil older ones down to a few lines, so the folder stays thin without losing the thread.

CompactionShrinking older history so the conversation stays inside its token budget.
