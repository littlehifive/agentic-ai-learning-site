18.5.2 Plan-and-execute

Map the whole route, then drive it

The ReAct loop decides one step at a time. Plan-and-execute does the opposite: it writes the full list of steps up front, then runs them in order. If a step fails or surprises it, it re-plans from where it is.

A surprise on the road? Re-plan and keep going
Think of it like a road trip with a printed route. You map every turn before leaving, which is efficient. But when a road is closed, you pull over and re-route from your current spot rather than guessing at every corner.
