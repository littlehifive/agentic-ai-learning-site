9.4 When to Use PRMs

Pick the grader that matches the task

Reach for an ORM when

  • The task is simple and the answer is what matters, such as sentiment, toxicity, or a factual check.
  • Step-by-step labels would be expensive and add little.
  • You want the cheapest option to label and to run at scale.
  • There is no long chain of reasoning to grade in between.

Reach for a PRM when

  • The task needs multi-step reasoning, such as maths, code, or logic.
  • The final answer is just right or wrong, but the steps vary in quality.
  • You want to guide a search, scoring partial solutions as they grow.
  • You can get step-level labels, by hand or generated automatically.
Rule of thumb: simple task, use an ORM because it is cheaper and good enough. Long reasoning, use a PRM because dense step feedback is worth the extra cost and is harder for the model to game.
