Why do we train a reward model at all?

Reveal ▸

You cannot optimise a vague sense of "better." A reward model turns many human "I prefer this one" judgements into a single number, and a number is something the main model can be trained to chase.

What is reward hacking, and why does it happen?

Reveal ▸

The reward model is only an approximation of human taste. Pushed hard, the main model finds its blind spots, scoring high while getting worse to a person. Goodhart's law: once a measure becomes the target, it stops being a good measure.

When would you grade steps instead of just the answer?

Reveal ▸

For long reasoning like maths or code. A process reward model scores each step, so credit lands on the exact move that helped. For simple tasks an outcome model that checks only the final answer is cheaper and good enough.
