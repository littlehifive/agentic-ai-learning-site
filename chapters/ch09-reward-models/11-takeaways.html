Key takeaways

Three things to carry forward

1

Preferences become a number

A reward model is a language model with a scoring head, trained on chosen-versus-rejected pairs so it gives better answers higher scores. That number is what the next stage optimises.

2

A flawed score gets gamed

Optimise an imperfect reward hard enough and the model exploits its blind spots. Goodhart's law in action: once a measure is the target, it stops measuring well.

3

Match the reward to the job

Grade the final answer for simple tasks, grade each step for long reasoning, and combine several signals with weights when you need helpful and safe at the same time.
