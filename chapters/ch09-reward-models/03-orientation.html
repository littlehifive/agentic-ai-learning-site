Chapter 9 · Reward Model Training

The reward model is the middle link in aligning a model

Aligning a model to human taste happens in three stages. The reward model is stage two: it learns what people prefer, then stands in as an automatic judge while the main model practises against it.

Stage 1

Collect preferences

People are shown two answers to the same prompt and pick the better one. This builds a dataset of chosen-versus-rejected pairs.

→

Stage 2 · this chapter

Train the reward model

A model learns to give the chosen answer a higher score than the rejected one, turning preferences into numbers.

→

Stage 3

Optimise the main model

The main model generates answers, the reward model scores them, and the main model is nudged toward higher-scoring answers.
