9.6 Multi-Objective Rewards

Most answers must be helpful and safe at once

A single score rarely captures everything you care about. You usually want an answer that is helpful, but also safe, honest, and not too long. The fix is to train several reward signals and combine them, with weights that say how much each one counts.

Think of it like judging a restaurant. The food matters most, but cleanliness and price count too. You weight each one and add them up. Weight taste at zero and you would happily eat in a filthy kitchen, so the weights encode what you truly value.

In plain terms

Weighted sumThe simplest recipe: multiply each reward by its weight and add them together.
Normalise firstPut every reward on the same scale before adding, so a big-numbered one does not drown the rest.
The trade-offRaising one weight, like safety, usually costs a little on another, like helpfulness.
