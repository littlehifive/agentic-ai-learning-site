1.1 How LLMs work: an intuitive overview

Four steps, repeated: read, understand, predict, append

Step 1

Tokenize

Chop the text into small reusable pieces called tokens.

→

Step 2

Embed

Turn each token into a list of numbers that captures its meaning.

→

Step 3

Process

Stacked layers let every token take account of all the others.

→

Step 4

Predict

Score every possible next token and pick one.

Add the chosen token to the text and run the whole thing again

In plain terms

TokenA small chunk of text, often part of a word, that the model treats as one unit.
AutoregressiveIt generates one token at a time, feeding each new token back in to predict the next.
