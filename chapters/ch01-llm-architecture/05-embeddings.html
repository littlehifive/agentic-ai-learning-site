1.3 Embeddings: from words to numbers

Each token becomes a point in a map of meaning

A token on its own is just an ID. The model converts it into a list of numbers, an embedding, that places it in a space where related meanings sit close together. The model learns these positions from data.

royalty king queen prince fruit apple banana mango
Think of it like a map where words you would use in the same sentence end up in the same neighbourhood. "You know a word by the company it keeps."

In plain terms

EmbeddingA list of numbers (a vector) that stands for a token's meaning.
DimensionsHow long that list is, often a few thousand numbers; more room means finer distinctions.
