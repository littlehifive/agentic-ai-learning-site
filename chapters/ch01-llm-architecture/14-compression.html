1.14 Model compression

Three ways to make a model smaller and faster

A trained model can be large and slow to run. These methods shrink it for cheaper deployment, usually with little loss in quality.

Quantization

Store each number with fewer digits of precision. The model gets much smaller and faster, and quality barely moves.

Like writing prices as whole dollars instead of to the cent: far less to store, still close enough.

Knowledge distillation

Train a small "student" model to copy a large "teacher" model's behaviour, inheriting much of its skill in a fraction of the size.

Like an apprentice learning by watching an expert, then doing the job alone.

Pruning

Remove parts of the network that contribute little, like trimming dead branches, leaving a leaner model.

Like editing a draft down to only the words that carry weight.

PrecisionHow many digits are used to store each number; fewer digits means a smaller, faster model.
