Key takeaways

Three things to carry forward

1

One loop underneath

A model just predicts the next token, over and over. Tokenize, embed, attend, predict, repeat. Every other idea modifies one of those steps.

2

Most progress is efficiency

Flash Attention, GQA, LoRA, quantization, and Mixture of Experts all chase the same goal: the same capability for less memory, time, or money.

3

A few knobs decide success

The learning rate, the optimizer, and the decoding method shape the outcome more than almost anything else. Get those right first.
