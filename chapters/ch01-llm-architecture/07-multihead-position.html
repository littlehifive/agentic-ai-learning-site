1.3.6 to 1.3.7 Multi-head attention and position

Many viewpoints at once, plus a sense of word order

Multi-head attention

Instead of paying attention one way, the model runs several attention operations in parallel. Each "head" learns to track a different kind of relationship, and their results are combined.

grammar subject → verb meaning pronoun → noun nearby words

Positional encoding (RoPE)

Attention by itself ignores order, yet "dog bites man" and "man bites dog" differ. The model adds position information so it knows where each word sits. The common method today, RoPE, encodes position by rotating each word's numbers by an amount that depends on its place in the sentence.
