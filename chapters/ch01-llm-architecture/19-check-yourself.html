Why does a model only look backward at earlier words?

Reveal ▸

Because it generates one word at a time. A causal mask hides future words, so each new word is predicted only from what came before, which is exactly how writing left to right works.

What problem does Flash Attention solve?

Reveal ▸

Plain attention shuffles a huge grid of scores through slow memory. Flash Attention does the same math in small tiles in the chip's fast memory, so it runs 2 to 4 times faster with the same result.

Why use LoRA instead of retraining the whole model?

Reveal ▸

Retraining everything is slow and costly. LoRA freezes the original model and learns a tiny add-on, capturing the new skill for under one percent of the size, and you can keep different add-ons for different tasks.
