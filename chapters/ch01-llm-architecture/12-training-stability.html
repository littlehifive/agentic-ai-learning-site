1.5 Keeping training stable

Four safeguards that stop training from going off the rails

Large models are delicate to train. These standard habits keep the process smooth, fast, and within the memory budget.

WarmupStart with tiny steps and ramp up, so early, shaky updates do not destabilize the model.
ScheduleTake large steps early, then shrink them over time as the model settles near a good solution.
Gradient clippingCap any single update that is too large, preventing a rare spike from wrecking progress.
Mixed precisionStore numbers with fewer digits to save memory and run faster, while protecting the parts that need accuracy.

In plain terms

GradientThe direction and size of the suggested adjustment at each step.
BF16 vs FP16Two 16-bit number formats. BF16 covers a wider range and is the safer default; FP16 needs extra care to avoid errors.
