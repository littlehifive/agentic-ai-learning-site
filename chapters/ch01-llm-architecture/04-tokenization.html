1.2 Tokenization

First, text is broken into reusable pieces

Models do not read letters or whole words. They use subword pieces: common words stay whole, while rare words split into familiar fragments. This keeps the vocabulary manageable and lets the model handle words it has never seen.

"cryptocurrency" → crypt ocur rency "the" → the
Think of it like Lego bricks for language. A small set of pieces can be snapped together to spell almost any word, so the model never hits a word it cannot represent.

In plain terms

VocabularyThe fixed set of pieces a model knows, usually 32,000 to 128,000 of them.
SubwordA piece smaller than a word; the sweet spot between single letters and whole words.
BPE (Byte-Pair Encoding)The common recipe for choosing pieces: repeatedly merge the most frequent neighbouring pair.
