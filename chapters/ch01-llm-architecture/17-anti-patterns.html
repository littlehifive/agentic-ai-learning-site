Anti-patterns · common mistakes

Four habits that quietly waste effort

1 Ignoring the memory cost of attention. On long inputs the naive approach becomes painfully slow. Use Flash Attention, which is the standard fix.
2 Reaching for full retraining first. It is expensive and often unnecessary. Start with LoRA; only go further if it falls short.
3 Skipping the learning-rate search. The wrong value breaks training no matter what else you do. Tune it before anything else.
4 Forcing step-by-step reasoning everywhere. On simple questions it just adds delay and can introduce mistakes. Save it for genuinely hard problems.
