1.10 Mixture of Experts (MoE)

A panel of specialists, with a router that picks a few

What to notice

  1. 1A small router looks at each word and picks just two experts to handle it; the other six stay idle.
  2. 2So the model can hold a lot of knowledge across many experts, while the work per word stays small.

In plain terms

ExpertOne of several sub-networks; different ones specialise in different inputs.
RouterThe chooser that sends each word to its best-matching experts.
