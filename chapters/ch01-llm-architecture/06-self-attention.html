1.3.5 Self-attention

Every word decides which other words matter

This is the heart of the Transformer. To understand a word, the model lets it look at the other words and pull in the ones that help. In "it" below, attention links back to what "it" refers to.

The cat sat because it was tired

The word "it" attends most to "the cat", so the model knows what "it" means.

Think of it like each word asking a question ("who here is relevant to me?"), every other word holding up a label, and the best matches handing over their content. Those three roles are the query, the key, and the value.

In plain terms

Query, Key, ValueQuery = what a word is looking for; Key = what each word offers; Value = the information passed along when they match.
Causal maskA rule that hides future words, so each word can only look backward. That is what makes generation work one token at a time.
