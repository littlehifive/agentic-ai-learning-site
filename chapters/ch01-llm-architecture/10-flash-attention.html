1.6 Flash Attention, and how attention got efficient

Each step removed the previous one's bottleneck

2017

Plain attention

The baseline. Comparing every word with every other builds a huge grid of scores that is slow to move in and out of memory.

→grid too big

2022

Flash Attention

Fixes memory traffic. Does the work in small tiles on the chip's fast memory, never writing the full grid out. Same answer, 2 to 4 times faster.

→cache too big

2023

Grouped-Query Attention

Fixes the running memory. Shares keys and values across heads, shrinking the per-conversation cache about fourfold.

→long text

Now

Sparse and windowed attention

Fixes long-context cost. Lets each word look at only the most relevant others, and stacks on top of Flash Attention.

In plain terms

Fast vs slow memoryA chip has a tiny fast workspace (SRAM) and a big slow store (HBM). Flash Attention keeps the heavy work in the fast workspace.
Exact, not approximateIt gives the same result as plain attention; only the bookkeeping changes, so there is no quality cost.
