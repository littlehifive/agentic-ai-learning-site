1.9 LoRA and parameter-efficient fine-tuning

Adapt a giant model by training a tiny add-on

Fully retraining a large model is expensive. LoRA freezes the original and learns a small set of extra numbers alongside it, capturing the new skill at a fraction of the cost. The add-on can even be merged back in so it adds no slowdown.

Think of it like sticky notes in a textbook. You leave the printed pages untouched and just add a few notes; cheap to write, easy to swap out, and you can keep different note sets for different tasks.
