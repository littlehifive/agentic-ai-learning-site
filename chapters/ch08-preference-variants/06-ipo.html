8.3 IPO · Identity Preference Optimization

Stop once the gap is big enough

The problem it fixes: DPO has no natural stopping point. It keeps widening the gap between the chosen and rejected answer, pushing the good one toward certain and the bad one toward impossible. With imperfect labels that turns into memorizing the training set. IPO targets a specific, finite gap and is happy once it gets there.

Think of it like a thermostat instead of a stuck-open heater. DPO heats the room forever; IPO aims for a set temperature and holds it, so one mislabeled example cannot drag the model to an extreme.

In plain terms

The fixAim for a fixed target margin between good and bad, rather than chasing an ever-larger one.
BonusA mislabeled pair gets only bounded influence, so the model generalizes instead of memorizing.
Reach for it whenYour labels are noisy, or DPO looks great on training data but worse on real tests.
