DPOMatched pairs: a good and a bad answer for the same prompt.
KTOJust single labels: one thumbs-up or thumbs-down per answer. Easiest data to get.
Online DPOFresh pairs the model makes itself, scored as it trains.
Best-of-NNo preference data at all; only a scorer to rank fresh answers.

Takeaway: if collecting matched pairs is the bottleneck, KTO asks the least of you.

DPO / KTO / IPOTwo models: the policy plus a frozen reference.
Online DPOThree models: policy, reference, and a reward model to judge fresh answers.
ORPO / SimPOOne model. No reference copy, so roughly half the memory.
Best-of-NOne model plus a scorer; no training machinery at all.

Takeaway: when memory is tight, the reference-free pair (ORPO, SimPO) is the lightest to train.
