Chapter 8 · Orientation

Five fixes, one shared starting point

Read this as a menu, not a ladder. Each row names the one limitation of standard DPO that the variant is built to remove. You pick by which limitation is hurting you.

Online DPO
Fixes drift

DPO trains on stale pairs made by another model. Online DPO makes fresh pairs from the model as it learns.

KTO
Fixes the data shape

DPO needs matched good-and-bad pairs. KTO learns from plain thumbs-up or thumbs-down on single answers.

IPO
Fixes overfitting

DPO can keep pushing forever and memorize noisy labels. IPO aims for a fixed, sensible gap and stops.

ORPO · SimPO
Fix the extra model

DPO keeps a second reference model in memory. Both of these drop it, so you train with one model.

Best-of-N
Fixes the complexity

No alignment training at all. Generate several answers and keep the best one a scorer picks.
