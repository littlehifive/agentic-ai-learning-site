8.1 Online DPO

Train on fresh answers, not stale ones

The problem it fixes: standard DPO learns from preference pairs written by some other model. As your model improves, it stops sounding like those old pairs, so the lesson no longer matches the student. Online DPO closes the gap by generating new pairs from the current model at every step and having a scorer pick the better one.

Think of it like coaching a player with footage of this week's game instead of last season's. The feedback lands because it is about how they play now.

In plain terms

The fixMake new preference pairs from the model itself as training goes, so the data never goes out of date.
The priceYou now need a reward model to judge the fresh answers, and generating them costs extra time.
Reach for it whenPlain DPO has stopped improving and you want the model to keep exploring past its dataset.
