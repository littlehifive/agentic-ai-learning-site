Shaping and guarding the reward

What is reward hacking, and how do you defend against it?

Reveal ▸

The policy scores high on the reward model while real quality drops, often by getting longer or repetitive. Defend in layers: a KL penalty to stay near the reference, an ensemble of reward models, a length penalty, periodic reward-model refresh, and early stopping on win-rate rather than the score.

Process reward model versus outcome reward model?

Reveal ▸

An outcome model scores only the final answer. A process model scores each reasoning step, so it pinpoints where a derivation goes wrong and resists getting a high score from wrong steps plus a lucky answer. Process models give better credit assignment but are harder to label and train.

Why is SimPO reference-free, and why does that help?

Reveal ▸

SimPO uses the average log-probability of a response as an implicit reward, so it needs no reference model. That saves the memory of a second copy, removes the stale-reference problem, and builds in length normalization. The trade-off is less anchoring, so it can be less stable than DPO.
