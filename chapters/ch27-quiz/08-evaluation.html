Knowing if it actually improved

How do you tell if RL really improved the model?

Reveal ▸

No single number captures quality. Lead with win-rate against the SFT baseline judged blind on hundreds of prompts. Add capability benchmarks to catch regressions, plus safety and truthfulness checks. A rising reward score with flat win-rate means hacking, not improvement.

What is position bias in LLM-as-judge, and how do you fix it?

Reveal ▸

An LLM judge tends to favor whichever response is first or last, regardless of quality. Mitigate by judging each pair both ways and taking the majority, by using a panel of judges, or by scoring each response against a rubric instead of comparing them side by side.

What is benchmark contamination, and how do you catch it?

Reveal ▸

Contamination is when test items leak into training data, inflating scores. Detect it with n-gram overlap checks, canary strings, or rephrased benchmarks where a big accuracy drop reveals memorization. Defend with dynamic and private test sets and decontamination during training.
