Three ways to align a model

Why does PPO's clipped objective beat vanilla policy gradient?

Reveal ▸

Vanilla gradients let one extreme sample push the policy into a bad region it cannot recover from. PPO clips the new-over-old probability ratio to a narrow band, so each update changes the policy by at most about 20 percent. It is a cheap first-order stand-in for a trust region.

What is the core idea behind DPO?

Reveal ▸

DPO rewrites the RLHF objective so the reward is expressed through the policy itself, which lets the partition function cancel. You then train directly on preference pairs with a simple contrastive loss. Same goal as RLHF, no separate reward model, but it assumes clean, transitive preferences.

GRPO versus PPO: when do you pick each?

Reveal ▸

GRPO drops the value model and scores a group of samples by above or below the group mean, ideal for verifiable right-or-wrong rewards. PPO keeps a value function for fine per-token credit, better for nuanced reward-model scores. Verifiable means GRPO, nuanced and max quality means PPO.
