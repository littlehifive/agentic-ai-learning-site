5.7 The moving parts: networks in play

PPO keeps four models working together

This is what makes PPO heavy to run. At training time, four separate models are in memory at once, each with a single job. Only one of them actually changes.

Learns

Policy

The model being trained. It writes the answers and is the only one whose weights are updated.

Updated every step

Anchor

Reference

A frozen copy of where the model started. It marks home base so the policy does not drift unrecognisably far.

Frozen

Judges

Reward model

A separate scorer that rates each finished answer. It stands in for human taste about what is good.

Frozen

Predicts

Value model, the critic

Guesses how well things will go from each point, providing the bar that the advantage is measured against.

Updated alongside

Think of it like a player coached during a match: the player improves, a recording of their old form keeps them recognisable, a judge scores each play, and a commentator predicts what a normal play is worth.

In plain terms

CriticAnother name for the value model: it criticises by saying whether an outcome beat expectations.
FrozenA model held fixed during training, used only to score or to anchor, never updated.
