5.4 The advantage function

Ask of each choice: better or worse than expected?

A raw reward says how good the whole answer was, but not which choices deserve the credit. PPO keeps a running guess of how well things usually go from each point, then asks whether a choice beat that guess. That gap, the advantage, is what the update actually pushes on.

+

Positive advantage

The choice did better than the usual outcome from here. Make it a little more likely next time.

−

Negative advantage

The choice did worse than expected. Make it a little less likely, even if the answer overall was fine.

Think of it like grading a student against their own expected score, not the class average. Beating your own par means you did something right; falling short means that particular move hurt, regardless of the final grade.

In plain terms

AdvantageHow much a choice did better, or worse, than the model expected at that moment.
Value estimateThe model's running guess of how well things will go from a given point; the bar to beat.
WhiteningRescaling the advantages each batch so the signal is steady and not dominated by outliers.
