5.6 PPO for RLHF: the KL penalty

A leash keeps the model close to where it started

The clip limits each single step, but many small steps in the same direction still add up to a long drift. So PPO adds a second guard: a penalty that grows the further the model strays from its frozen starting point. Chase reward, yes, but pay a toll for wandering off.

What it measures

At each token, PPO compares the trained model with the reference: how differently are they speaking now? That gap is the KL distance. A small steady value means the model is still itself, just better.

Why it matters

Without the leash, the model learns to please the scorer in weird ways and forgets how to talk. The penalty keeps fluency and knowledge intact while reward gently tilts behaviour in the wanted direction.

Think of it like a dog on a long leash chasing a scent. It can roam and follow the reward, but the leash quietly pulls back the further it gets, so it never bolts out of sight of home.

In plain terms

KL distanceA measure of how far the trained model's choices have moved from the reference model's.
KL penaltyA cost added to the reward that grows with that distance, discouraging drift.
Reference modelThe frozen starting model that the leash is anchored to.
