Why does PPO clip how much an update can change the model?

Reveal ▸

Because an unconstrained step can shove the model into a bad region it cannot recover from. Clipping caps each change, usually to about twenty percent, so a single noisy batch can nudge the model but never wreck it.

What does the advantage tell you that a raw reward does not?

Reveal ▸

The reward says how good the whole answer was; the advantage says whether each particular choice did better or worse than expected. That is what the update pushes on, so credit lands on the choices that actually mattered.

Why must PPO throw away its answers after each cycle?

Reveal ▸

PPO is on-policy: its safety math compares the new model with the one that produced the answers. Once the model has changed, the old answers no longer match it, the comparison goes stale, and the clipping guarantee breaks. So fresh answers are needed every cycle.
