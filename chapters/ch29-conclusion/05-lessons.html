29.1 Summary, the themes that recur

Lessons that show up in every chapter

How to build

  • Simplicity scales. Use the simplest design that meets the need. Reach for chains and routing before autonomous loops, single agents before swarms.
  • There is no single best method. PPO, DPO, and GRPO each trade quality for cost. The right pick depends on your data, budget, and quality bar.
  • Standards unlock ecosystems. MCP and A2A are to agents what HTTP was to the web: shared plumbing that lets separate pieces work together.

What makes it work

  • Learning signals get richer. From human ratings to verifiable rewards, reasoning can emerge from a simple signal applied at scale.
  • Evaluation drives everything. Without rigorous measurement, progress is invisible and regressions slip through unnoticed.
  • Agents are the natural next step. Once a model is aligned, the question shifts from one good answer to solving multi-step problems on its own.
The author lists seven themes in all. Complexity should be earned through demonstrated need, never added by default.
