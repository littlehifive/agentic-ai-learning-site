29.1 Summary

The journey, layer by layer

Chs 1 to 2Foundations. How a transformer is built and served: tokenize, embed, attend, predict, repeat, on real hardware.
Chs 3 to 9Learning from feedback. Reinforcement learning for alignment: PPO, DPO, GRPO, preference variants, and reward models.
Chs 10 to 11Training in practice. Supervised fine-tuning, then the infrastructure that scales it across hundreds of GPUs.
Chs 12 to 14Reasoning and proof. Agentic training, reasoning models that think before answering, and evaluation that keeps them honest.
Chs 15 to 18Becoming an agent. The agent loop, retrieval, memory, and the harness that turns a model into a worker.
Chs 19 to 23Patterns and protocols. Design patterns, safe environments, plus MCP, skills, and A2A as the connective tissue.
Chs 24 to 26Many agents. Multi-agent systems, the frameworks that run them, and the user interfaces around them.
Read top to bottom, each stage assumes the one above it. The agent at the end is the foundation model, plus everything in between.
