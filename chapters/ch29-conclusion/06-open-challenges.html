29.2 The road ahead

What is still unsolved

Learning from interaction

Today alignment is a one-time training phase. The goal: agents that keep improving from every interaction without forgetting or drifting.

Scalable oversight

As models exceed human understanding, how do people still judge them? Ideas: AI helping humans evaluate, debate, and rewarding reasoning steps.

World models and planning

Agents react one step at a time. Future ones need to predict the consequences of actions and search ahead before they act.

Reliability and evaluation

Benchmarks test isolated tasks. Real work brings vague goals and recovery. Evaluation must also become cost-aware and safe under new inputs.

Security and trust

Tool access and shared memory open new attacks. Prompt injection has no robust defense yet, and tracing who authorized what stays unsolved.

Cost and efficiency

Training and running agents is expensive. Broad impact needs distillation, leaner algorithms, on-device agents, and strong open-weight models.
