Key takeaways

Three things to carry with you

1

It all stacks

An agent is not a different thing from a language model. It is the model plus training, alignment, tools, and memory, each layer built on the one below.

2

Prefer the simplest thing

Reach for the plainest design that meets the need, and earn complexity only when you can show it is required. Then measure everything you build.

3

The field is young

Oversight, security, planning, and cost are all still open. The map you now hold is also a list of problems worth working on.
