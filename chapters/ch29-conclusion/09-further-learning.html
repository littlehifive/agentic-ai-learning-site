29.3 Further reading

Where to go from here

Foundations

  • Attention Is All You Need, the transformer.
  • InstructGPT, the first large RLHF deployment.
  • PPO, DPO, GRPO, the alignment methods.
  • RAG, retrieval-augmented generation.

Agentic AI

  • ReAct and Toolformer, reasoning plus tools.
  • Building Effective Agents, design patterns.
  • SWE-bench, OSWorld, GAIA, real-task tests.
  • MCP and A2A, the connecting protocols.

Tools to try

  • HuggingFace TRL, a production RL library.
  • LangGraph and AutoGen, agent workflows.
  • DSPy, declarative prompt optimization.
  • OpenAI Agents SDK, an agent framework.
The book lists dozens more. Start with one paper and one tool, then follow the references that matter to your own work.
