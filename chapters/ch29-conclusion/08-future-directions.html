29.2 The road ahead

The directions the field is heading

✓ From one-time training to continuous learning. Agents that improve safely from deployment, not just from a fixed dataset.
✓ From reacting to planning. Internal world models that let an agent look ahead before it commits to an action.
✓ From single models to ecosystems. Open protocols so agents from different teams can find and trust each other.
✓ From humans grading every output to scalable oversight. Using AI, debate, and step-level rewards to keep up with capable models.
✓ From benchmarks to real deployment metrics. Cost-aware, safety-aware evaluation that survives messy, shifting tasks.
✓ From costly cloud runs to broad access. Smaller distilled agents, leaner algorithms, and capable open-weight models.
Each direction turns a current limit into the next research frontier. The arc that built this field is the one that will keep extending it.
