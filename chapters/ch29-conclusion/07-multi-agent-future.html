29.2.4 A future direction

Where it points: an economy of agents

The author sees a future where hundreds of specialized agents, built by different teams, collaborate, negotiate, and hand work off to each other. The A2A protocol is an early hint of this. But an economy needs more than connections: it needs trust between strangers, rules for sharing limited resources, and a clear answer to who is responsible when a chain of agents causes harm.

Think of it like a city of contractors. One agent hires another, who subcontracts a third. The work gets done fast, but only if there are contracts, reputations, and courts. Today the agents exist; the contracts and courts do not.

In plain terms

DelegationOne agent handing part of a task to another that is better suited for it.
Trust calibrationAn agent learning when not to believe a tool result or another agent's claim.
GovernanceThe rules and accountability for who answers when a group of agents does something harmful.
