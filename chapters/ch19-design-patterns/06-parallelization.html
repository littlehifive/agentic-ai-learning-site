19.1.3 Workflow patterns: parallelization

Run independent subtasks at once, then combine

When parts of a job do not depend on each other, run them side by side and stitch the results together. The total wait is the slowest single call, not the sum of all of them.

Security reviewScan the code for vulnerabilities.
Performance reviewLook for slow paths and waste.
Style reviewCheck naming and readability.
→

Aggregate

Merge findings, remove duplicates, rank by severity.

Think of it like three reviewers reading the same document at the same time, each for a different thing, then meeting once to pool their notes. Far faster than one person doing all three passes in turn.

In plain terms

SectioningSplit the input into separate chunks and process each on its own.
VotingAsk the same question several times, then pick the best or most common answer.
