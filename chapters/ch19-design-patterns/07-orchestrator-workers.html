19.1.4 Toward autonomy: orchestrator-workers

A lead model splits the work among workers

Here the model itself decides how to break up the job. An orchestrator studies the task, writes a plan of subtasks, hands each to a worker, then merges the results. The key shift: the breakdown is decided by the model, not hard-coded by you.

Orchestrator

Reads the task and writes a plan of subtasks.

→
Worker AMap the dependencies.
Worker BEdit the affected files.
Worker CUpdate the tests.
→

Synthesize

Merge worker outputs into one coherent result.

Think of it like a general contractor on a remodel. They size up the job, decide which trades are needed, hire the plumber and electrician, then make sure the finished rooms fit together. You did not list the trades in advance.
