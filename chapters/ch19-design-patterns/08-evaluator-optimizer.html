19.1.5 Toward autonomy: evaluator-optimizer

One model drafts, another critiques, loop until good

A two-model feedback loop. A generator produces a candidate; a separate evaluator scores it against explicit criteria. If it falls short, the critique is fed back to the generator and the cycle repeats, until the quality bar is met or a retry budget runs out.

Generator

Produces a candidate output, then revises it using the latest critique.

→draft

Evaluator

Scores the draft against clear criteria and writes a critique of what to fix.

If the score is too low, send the critique back to the generator and try again
Think of it like a writer and an editor passing a draft back and forth. The editor marks up what is weak, the writer revises, and they repeat until the piece clears the bar or the deadline forces them to stop.
