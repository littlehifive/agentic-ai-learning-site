19.1.1 Workflow patterns: prompt chaining

Break a task into a fixed sequence of steps

The simplest pattern. Split a complex job into a set order of model calls, and feed each step's output into the next. A gate between steps can check the work and stop errors before they spread downstream.

Step 1

Draft outline

Turn the request into a structured outline.

→gate

Step 2

Write draft

Expand the outline into full prose.

→gate

Step 3

Polish

Tighten the wording to a final result.

Think of it like an assembly line. Each station does one job and hands the part to the next, and a quick inspection between stations catches a defect before more work is wasted on it.

In plain terms

GateA check between steps; pass it to continue, fail it to retry or stop.
Best forNaturally sequential work: content pipelines, multi-stage data transforms.
