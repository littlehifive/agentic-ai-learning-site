19.1 to 19.2 The taxonomy of building blocks

The whole catalogue splits into two families

Every pattern in this chapter is a way to wire up one or more model calls. They fall into two groups by who controls the order of work: the system you wrote, or the model itself.

Workflows

You hard-wire the control flow. The system, not the model, decides the execution order.

  • Prompt chaining · a fixed sequence of steps
  • Routing · classify, then send to the right handler
  • Parallelization · run independent subtasks at once
Toward autonomy

The model takes a hand on the wheel, deciding how to split or refine the work.

  • Orchestrator-workers · the model splits the work itself
  • Evaluator-optimizer · draft, critique, repeat until good
  • Fully autonomous agents · the model runs its own loop
