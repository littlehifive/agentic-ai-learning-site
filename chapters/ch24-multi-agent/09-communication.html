24.3.2 & 24.4 Communication protocols

Agents coordinate by sending clear messages

The most natural way for language-model agents to work together is to send each other structured text. To stay reliable, those messages need an agreed shape, and the system needs a rule for how much past conversation to forward.

What a message says

  • Inform share a finding.
  • Request ask another agent to do something.
  • Propose, accept, reject negotiate a plan.
  • Confirm, failure report done, or report that it broke.

How much history to share

  • Full history for short chats; most informative, but fills the context window.
  • Summary for medium chats; cheaper, but can drop details.
  • Relevant excerpt for long sessions; fetch only the messages that matter.
Think of it like a well-run email thread. A clear subject line and request type means the reader knows what to do at a glance, and you do not forward the entire thread when a one-line summary will do.

In plain terms

Message passingAgents coordinate by sending each other structured text messages.
PerformativeA label on a message saying what it is for: inform, request, propose, and so on.
Context sharingThe choice of how much prior conversation to pass to the next agent.
