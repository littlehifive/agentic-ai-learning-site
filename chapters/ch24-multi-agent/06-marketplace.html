24.3.5 Market-based coordination

Agents bid for the work they are best at

Instead of a manager assigning every job, the work is auctioned. A task is announced, agents that can do it submit bids saying how well and how cheaply they could, and the best bid wins. This is the Contract Net Protocol, one of the oldest coordination ideas in the field, now run with language-model agents.

1

Announce

A manager broadcasts a task with its requirements to all agents.

2

Bid

Agents reply with what they can do and an estimated cost, for example "I can finish this in three steps with high confidence."

3

Award

The manager hands the contract to the best bidder.

4

Deliver

The winner does the work and reports back the result.

Think of it like a contractor putting a job out to tender. Builders bid on price and ability; the cheapest capable bid wins. Useful when budget is tight, because the market steers work toward the agent that can do it for the least.

In plain terms

BidAn agent's offer: what it can do, how well, and at what cost.
Contract NetAnnounce, bid, award, deliver; a task auction among agents.
Best forCost-conscious settings where you want work routed to the cheapest capable agent.
