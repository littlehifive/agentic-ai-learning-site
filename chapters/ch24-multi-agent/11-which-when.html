24.10 Architecture comparison

Match the shape to the shape of the task

No architecture wins everywhere. Pick by what the task actually needs: speed, traceability, resilience, or scale. A quick guide.

Start here for control

  • Supervisor for small teams and clear task splits; simplest to build and easiest to debug.
  • Pipeline for sequential work with clear stages, like research then outline then draft then edit.
  • Ensemble for high-stakes decisions; several agents solve it, pick the best, trade compute for reliability.

Reach for these to scale

  • Hierarchical for complex, multi-domain enterprise work; the usual choice in production.
  • Swarm for simple handoff chains like customer-service routing, and many agents.
  • Decentralized for dynamic, resilience-critical settings where no single point may fail.
Rules of thumb Fewer than five agents: a supervisor is simplest. Fault tolerance matters: avoid a central hub. Debuggability matters: keep it centralized or a pipeline. Most production systems settle on hierarchical.
