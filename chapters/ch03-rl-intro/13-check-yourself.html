What is the TD error, and why call it surprise?

Reveal ▸

It is the gap between what the agent expected and what actually happened plus what it now expects next. A positive gap means better than predicted, so raise that state's value; a negative gap means worse, so lower it. Drive the gap to zero and the estimates have settled.

Why do language models use policy methods, not Q-learning?

Reveal ▸

Q-learning has to score every possible action to pick the best. A language model has tens of thousands of actions, the whole vocabulary, at every token. Scoring them all per step is intractable, so LLMs tune the policy directly instead.

What does the lambda dial in GAE control?

Reveal ▸

The balance between bias and variance in the advantage estimate. Near 0 it trusts the critic: steady but biased. Near 1 it trusts real returns: accurate but noisy. Around 0.95 is the sweet spot used for language models.
