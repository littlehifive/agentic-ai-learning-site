3.7 Evolution to PPO for LLMs

Each step removed the previous one's noise problem

1992

REINFORCE

The baseline. Reward the whole run, then make good runs more likely. Works, but one trajectory per update makes the signal extremely noisy.

→too noisy

2016

A2C, add a critic

Fixes the noise. A critic rates each move on the spot and supplies an advantage baseline, so updates judge "better than average" with far less variance.

→bias vs variance

2016

GAE

Fixes the trade-off. One dial, lambda, blends short and long lookaheads, balancing a flawed critic against chaotic returns for the best advantage estimate.

→unsafe steps

2017

PPO

Fixes step size. Clips how far the policy can move in one update, so training stays stable. The standard for aligning language models.

In plain terms

The through-lineEvery step in this chain attacks the same enemy: noisy, unstable updates. Less variance, safer steps, faster learning.
Where it leadsPPO is the actor-critic recipe Part II uses to align LLMs; later GRPO drops the critic entirely for verifiable rewards.
