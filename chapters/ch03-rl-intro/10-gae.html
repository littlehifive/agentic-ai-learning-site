3.8 Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE)

One dial to balance steady against accurate

Estimating the advantage forces a choice. Trust the critic and look only one step ahead: very steady, but wrong if the critic is off. Trust the actual rewards all the way to the end: always accurate, but wildly noisy. GAE blends every horizon at once with a single knob, lambda, that slides between the two.

λ = 0

Trust the critic

One step ahead only. Low variance, steady updates, but biased if the critic is inaccurate. Can miss long-delayed rewards.

λ = 0.95

The sweet spot

Mostly trusts the critic but corrects with real returns for distant effects. The standard choice for training language models.

λ = 1

Trust the rewards

Full real returns to the end. Unbiased but very high variance. Updates can swing hard and destabilise training.

In plain terms

Bias vs varianceBias is leaning on a flawed critic; variance is leaning on a chaotic environment. GAE trades one for the other smoothly.
Credit assignmentIt decides how much a reward now should credit the actions that led here. Higher lambda spreads credit further back in time.
