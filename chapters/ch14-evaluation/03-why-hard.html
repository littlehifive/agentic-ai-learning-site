14 · Why Evaluation is Hard for LLMs

Classic machine learning had an answer key. Language does not

In ordinary machine learning you compare a prediction to a known label and count how often it matched. With a language model, three things break that clean picture all at once.

1

No single right answer

A model can produce any string. Most useful questions have many good answers and no fixed key to check against.

2

Quality has many axes

Helpful, factual, safe, coherent, and well-styled are separate goals. Pushing one up can pull another down.

3

Judging is itself hard

Deciding if an answer is good takes understanding, so the judge can fail in the same ways the model does.

Think of it like grading a math quiz versus grading an essay. Math has a key and grades itself. An essay needs a thoughtful reader, and even two careful readers will disagree.

In plain terms

Intrinsic metricA cheap, fast score of the output on its own, good for quick iteration.
Extrinsic metricDid the model help the real task, measured in the field. Slow and costly, but it is what we actually care about.
