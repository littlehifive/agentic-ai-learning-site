14.1.2 When to Use What

Match the metric to the shape of the task

No metric is best everywhere. The right choice falls out of one question: does the task have a checkable answer, or is it a matter of judgment?

Checkable answer

  • Math or factual QA: exact match, the answer is right or it is not.
  • Code: pass@k, run it against the tests.
  • Agent task: task success rate, verify the goal state with a checker.
  • Quick model check: perplexity, a fast sanity signal during iteration.

Matter of judgment

  • Helpful, tone, creativity: human ratings, or a well-checked model judge.
  • Ranking many models: head-to-head votes turned into ELO or Bradley-Terry.
  • Large scale grading: LLM-as-judge, with bias controls in place.
  • Final call: extrinsic metrics, did real users get more done.
Think of it like choosing a measuring tool. You reach for a ruler for length and a thermometer for heat. Picking the metric is choosing the right instrument for what you actually want to know.
