14.1.2 Standard Benchmark Suites

A few public tests act as shared yardsticks

No single test covers everything, so teams report a panel of standard suites. Each one probes a different ability, and together they give a rounded, comparable picture across models. The trick is to read them as a set, not one headline number.

MMLU

Broad knowledge

Multiple-choice questions across dozens of subjects, from history to medicine. Tests general world knowledge.

HellaSwag

Common sense

Pick the sensible ending to an everyday situation. Tests whether the model has plain common sense.

HumanEval

Coding

Write a function that passes hidden tests. Scored with pass@k, so correctness is run, not guessed.

Think of it like a decathlon. You do not crown the champion on the sprint alone. You look across all the events, because strength in one says little about the rest.

In plain terms

Why a panelDifferent suites test different skills, so a spread of scores is more honest than any one.
Read with careA maxed-out suite may just be saturated, and contamination can quietly inflate any of them.
