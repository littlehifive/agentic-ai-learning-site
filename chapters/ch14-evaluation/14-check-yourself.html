Why is a lower perplexity not the same as a better assistant?

Reveal ▸

Perplexity only says the model was less surprised by some text. Being a good predictor of text is not the same as being helpful, accurate, or safe, which are the things users actually want.

What does pass@10 tell you that pass@1 does not?

Reveal ▸

Pass@1 is first-try reliability: one shot, did it work. Pass@10 allows ten attempts and counts success if any one passes, which is the right view when a person or tool can pick the working answer.

Why does benchmark contamination make a high score meaningless?

Reveal ▸

If the test questions were in the training data, the model can recall the answers rather than reason them out. The score measures memory of that test, not the skill it claims to, like a student who saw the exam early.
