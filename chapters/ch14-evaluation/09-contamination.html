14.8.1 Benchmark Contamination

When the test leaked into training, the score lies

Models learn from enormous slices of the internet, and benchmark questions live on the internet too. If the test questions ended up in the training data, the model can recall the answers instead of working them out. The score looks great and means nothing.

Test questions online

Public benchmarks sit in the same web text used to train models.

→

They slip into training

The model memorises the exact answers, not the underlying skill.

→

Inflated, hollow scores

High marks on the test, no real gain on new, unseen problems.

Think of it like a student who got the exam paper in advance. A perfect grade tells you nothing about whether they learned anything.

In plain terms

Detect itCheck for verbatim overlap with training text, or test on problems created after the training cutoff.
Prevent itKeep a private test set that is never published, and refresh benchmarks with fresh questions.
Related trapEven clean tests wear out as the field tunes against them, so treat any benchmark as a temporary signal.
