14.5.5 Perplexity

Perplexity: how surprised the model was by the text

Show a model some real text and ask, at each word, how confidently it predicted what actually came next. Perplexity bundles that into one number. Lower means less surprised, which means a better fit.

Predictable text

"The cat sat on the mat"

low · barely surprised, strong fit

Unfamiliar text

"The cat sat on the quark"

high · very surprised, poor fit

Think of it like a hesitation meter. Roughly, perplexity is how many words the model was wavering between at each step. Confident and right gives a low number, flustered gives a high one.

In plain terms

What it scoresHow well the model predicts a held-out piece of text it was not trained to memorise.
Best used forA quick sanity check and spotting drift, comparing the same model on the same text.
The catchA lower number is not automatically more helpful, and it cannot be compared across models with different tokenisers.
