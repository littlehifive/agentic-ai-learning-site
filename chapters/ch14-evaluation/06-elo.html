14.4.1 ELO Rating System

ELO: rank models by head-to-head wins, like chess

When there is no answer key, ask people to compare two answers and pick the better one. Each comparison is a match. Win and your rating climbs, lose and it falls, and the climb is larger when you beat a stronger opponent. The result is a leaderboard.

Think of it like chess ratings. You never score a player in the abstract. You watch who beats whom and let the numbers settle. A 400-point gap means the favourite wins about ten games out of eleven.

In plain terms

ELOUpdate ratings one match at a time. Simple and live, but the order of matches nudges the result.
Bradley-TerryFit one set of ratings to the whole match history at once. Preferred when you have the full record.
Chatbot ArenaA public arena that has gathered over one million human votes to rank chatbots this way.
