14.7 LLM-as-Judge

Use a strong model to grade other models

Human grading is slow and expensive. So hand the question, the answers, and a clear rubric to a capable model and let it score or pick a winner. It scales to huge test sets and can explain its reasoning.

Input

Question plus answers

Give the judge the prompt and one or two responses to assess.

→

Rubric

Clear criteria

Spell out what good looks like: accuracy, helpfulness, clarity.

→

Output

Score and reasons

A rating or a verdict, with a written rationale you can audit.

Think of it like a teaching assistant grading a stack of essays against a rubric. Fast and consistent, and right most of the time, but you still spot-check the grades because the assistant has habits of its own.

In plain terms

PointwiseScore one answer on its own, say 1 to 10.
PairwiseShow two answers and pick the better one, or call it a tie.
Validate itA judge is trusted when it agrees with humans on a held-out sample more than eight times in ten.
