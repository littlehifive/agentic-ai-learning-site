16.1.2 / 16.10 RAG, fine-tuning, and how they combine

RAG and fine-tuning solve different problems

Reach for RAG when

  • The knowledge changes often, or you need fresh facts
  • You want citations and traceable, grounded answers
  • The source is a large or private corpus of documents
  • You need to add or remove facts without retraining

Reach for fine-tuning when

  • You want to shape the style, tone, or output format
  • You need to teach a new skill or way of reasoning
  • Low latency matters and you cannot afford a lookup step
  • The behaviour, not the facts, is what must change
They are not rivals. Fine-tuning teaches the model how to reason and respond; RAG supplies what to reason about. The strongest systems do both: fine-tune the model to use retrieved context well, then feed it fresh, grounded passages.
