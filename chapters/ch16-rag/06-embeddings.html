16.2 / 16.3 Embedding and dense retrieval

Search by meaning, not matching words

Each chunk is turned into a list of numbers, an embedding, that captures its meaning. Passages about similar ideas land near each other. To search, the question becomes a list of numbers too, and the system returns the nearest passages.

"How do I reset my password?"the question, as a point → "Steps to recover account access"nearby, returned "Office holiday schedule"far away, skipped no shared keywords needed
Think of it like a map where related ideas sit close together. "Car" and "automobile" share no letters but live in the same neighbourhood, so a search for one finds the other. Keyword search would have missed it entirely.

In plain terms

EmbeddingA chunk turned into a list of numbers that encodes what it means.
Vector databaseA store built to find the nearest embeddings to a query fast, even among millions.
Cosine similarityThe usual measure of how close two embeddings point in the same direction.
