Why retrieve passages instead of just asking the model?

Reveal ▸

A plain model answers from frozen memory, so it invents facts, misses anything after its cutoff, and has never seen your private documents. Retrieval gives it current, citable text to ground the answer.

Why combine keyword search with meaning search?

Reveal ▸

Keyword search nails exact terms like codes and names; meaning search catches paraphrases and synonyms. Hybrid runs both and merges their rankings with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, so neither blind spot decides the result.

What does a reranker add after retrieval?

Reveal ▸

Fast retrieval is rough. A reranker reads the question and each top candidate together and re-scores them for true relevance, keeping only the best few. Slow per item, but you only run it on the shortlist.
