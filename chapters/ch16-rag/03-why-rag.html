16.1 Motivation and problem statement

A model's knowledge is baked in, and that is the problem

Everything a plain model knows is compressed into its weights during training. That stored knowledge is fixed, frozen at a date, and never includes your private documents. Three failures follow.

Hallucination

Past the edge of what it reliably knows, the model still answers confidently, producing statements that sound right but are simply wrong.

Staleness

Training data has a cutoff date. The model cannot know about events, releases, or papers that appeared after it was trained.

No private knowledge

A general model has never seen your codebase, internal policies, or specialised regulations, so it cannot reason over them.

Think of it like a brilliant scholar who memorised a huge library, then graduated years ago. RAG hands that scholar a library card so they can look things up and cite sources in real time, instead of reciting from an outdated memory.

In plain terms

Parametric knowledgeFacts stored inside the model's weights; fixed once training ends.
Non-parametric knowledgeFacts kept outside the model, in documents it can fetch and read on demand.
