25.3.5 DSPy · 25.3.6 Semantic Kernel

Two more, for special needs

Beyond the mainstream choices, two frameworks solve narrower problems well. One stops you hand-tuning prompts; the other fits agents into an existing enterprise software stack.

DSPy: stop tuning prompts by hand

You declare what each step should take in and give back, then provide a scoring metric and some examples. DSPy searches for the wording and examples that score best. It shines when you have a clear measure of success and enough data, and when you want to swap models without rewriting prompts.

Semantic Kernel: fit into the enterprise

Microsoft's enterprise-focused kit exposes your existing business logic as functions the agent can call, with strong support for the Microsoft and Azure stack, audit logging, and on-premises models. The natural pick when the agent must live inside established corporate software.

Think of it like specialist tools in a workshop. DSPy is a self-adjusting jig that finds the best setting for you; Semantic Kernel is the fitting that bolts cleanly onto machinery you already own.
