25.3.3 CrewAI

Staff a small team and give each one a job

CrewAI borrows the language of an org chart. You define each agent by a role, a goal, and a short backstory, then hand the crew a list of tasks. The model already understands how a researcher hands off to a writer, so describing the team in human terms is often enough to get useful collaboration with very little code.

Think of it like hiring a small agency. You write a job description and a brief for each person, line up the tasks, and let the team pass work down the line. You manage roles, not plumbing.

In plain terms

AgentA worker defined by a role, a goal, a backstory, and its tools.
TaskA specific assignment with a description and an expected output, given to one agent.
ProcessHow the crew runs: sequential (in order) or hierarchical (a manager delegates).
