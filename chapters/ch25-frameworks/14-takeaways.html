Key takeaways

Three things to carry forward

1

Don't rebuild the plumbing

Frameworks hand you the agent loop, state, tools, and orchestration. The choice is mostly about how much control you keep versus how much convenience you take.

2

Choose by your question

Need to steer every step? LangGraph. Agents that talk and run code? AutoGen. Least setup? CrewAI. On a maker's platform? Their first-party SDK.

3

Testing is not optional

Because agents are non-deterministic, you test behaviour in layers: tools, full loops, golden runs, and the rules. Skipping it just delays the failure.
