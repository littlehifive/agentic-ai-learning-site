25.8 Framework comparison

One spectrum: control on one end, convenience on the other

The frameworks are not rivals so much as points on a line. At one end you spell out every step and keep full control. At the other you describe the team in plain words and let the framework arrange the work. Where you sit is a trade, not a ranking.

More controlMore convenience

Spell it out

LangGraph

You draw the graph. Maximum control and visibility, more wiring to write.

Converse

AutoGen

You set up agents and let them chat. Flexible for collaboration and live code.

Describe the team

CrewAI

You name roles and tasks. Least boilerplate, least say over each step.

Think of it like web frameworks for agents. A bare toolkit gives you total control but you assemble everything; a batteries-included framework moves fast by deciding things for you. You are trading control for convenience either way.
