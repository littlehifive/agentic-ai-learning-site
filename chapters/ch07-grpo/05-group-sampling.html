7.2 Sample a group

One question, many attempts at once

GRPO starts every step by asking the model the same question several times over. Because the model samples a little randomly, the answers differ, giving a small spread of attempts to compare.

Think of it like handing the same exam question to five copies of the same student and collecting all five answers, instead of trusting a separate examiner to predict how they would each do.

In plain terms

Group size (G)How many answers are sampled per prompt; eight is the common default.
Why severalA single answer tells you nothing relative; a spread lets the model see what better and worse look like, side by side.
