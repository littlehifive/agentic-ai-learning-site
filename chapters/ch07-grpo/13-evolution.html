How the method evolved

Each step removed the previous one's bottleneck

2017

PPO

The baseline. Learns from rewards safely, but needs a second network, the critic, to judge each answer.

→critic too costly

2024

GRPO

Drops the critic. Compares a group of answers against their own average, removing a whole model and often improving stability.

→scaled to reasoning

2025

DeepSeek-R1

Proved the recipe. GRPO with simple right-or-wrong rewards drew out step-by-step reasoning all on its own.

→edge cases

Now

DAPO, GSPO, Dr. GRPO and more

Fixes the rough edges. A family of variants, each patching one failure mode of the basic group comparison.

In plain terms

The throughlineEvery step keeps the same safe, small-step update and changes only how the bar to beat is found.
Where it standsGRPO is now the default reinforcement-learning method across the major open-source training libraries.
