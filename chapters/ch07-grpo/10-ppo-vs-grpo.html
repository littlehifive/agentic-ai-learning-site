PPO vs GRPO

Same safe loop, one model lighter

GRPO is not a clean break from PPO. It keeps PPO's careful, small-step update and only swaps out where the bar to beat comes from.

PPO

  • Trains a second network, the critic, to predict each answer's score
  • That critic is the bar each choice is judged against
  • Two large models in memory at once: model plus critic
  • A wrong critic guess sends the update the wrong way
  • Generates one answer per prompt, then leans on the critic

GRPO

  • No critic at all; the group's average reward is the bar
  • That bar is a real measurement, not a learned guess
  • One model in memory: roughly half the footprint
  • An honest average cannot be miscalibrated the same way
  • Generates several answers per prompt and compares them
The one-line difference PPO asks a trained examiner to predict your grade; GRPO just hands out the same test five times and grades you on the curve.
