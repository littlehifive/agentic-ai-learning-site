7.2 Compare to the group

Graded on a curve, against the class average

Take the group's average reward as the bar. An answer's advantage is simply how far above or below that average it landed. There is no absolute target, only better-or-worse than the rest of the group.

Group average = the bar to beat Each answer is then scored relative to it.

+

Above the average

This answer beat its peers on the same question. Make the choices that led to it a little more likely next time.

−

Below the average

This answer lagged the rest of its group. Make those choices a little less likely, even if it was not terrible on its own.

Think of it like grading students on a curve. Nobody needs to know the absolute hardest possible score; you only need to know who beat the class average and who fell short of it.
