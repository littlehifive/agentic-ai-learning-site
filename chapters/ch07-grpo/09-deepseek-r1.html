The DeepSeek-R1 breakthrough

Reasoning that nobody taught it directly

DeepSeek introduced GRPO and then scaled it to their R1 model in 2024 to 2025. The headline result: trained with nothing but a right-or-wrong reward on math and code, the model grew real reasoning habits on its own.

It started to think aloud

Long chains of step-by-step working appeared, even though the reward only ever looked at the final answer.

It checked itself

The model began re-reading its own work and flagging doubtful steps, a habit no instruction asked for.

It fixed its mistakes

When a path went wrong, it learned to back up and try another, simply because that path scored better.

Why it mattered It showed that a simple, honest reward plus group comparison was enough to bring out reasoning, no hand-written examples of "how to think" required. This is what made GRPO the default for reasoning models.
