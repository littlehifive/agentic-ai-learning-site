What does GRPO use instead of PPO's critic?

Reveal ▸

A group of answers. It writes several answers to the same prompt, scores them, and uses their average reward as the bar each answer is judged against. No second network is trained.

Why is dropping the critic both cheaper and often more accurate?

Reveal ▸

Cheaper because the critic was about as big as the model itself, so removing it roughly halves memory. More accurate because a real measured average cannot be miscalibrated the way a half-trained guess can.

Why does a GRPO group need both wins and losses?

Reveal ▸

The signal comes from comparison. If every answer is right (or every one wrong), they all equal the average, so no answer is above or below it and there is nothing to push on. You need a spread to learn from.
