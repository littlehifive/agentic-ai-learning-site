7.2 The GRPO algorithm

The loop, end to end, in four moves

GRPO keeps the same safe, small-step update as PPO. The only change is where the bar to beat comes from: not a learned network, but the answers themselves.

1

Sample a group

Take one prompt and have the model write several answers to it, often eight at once.

2

Score each one

Give every answer a reward. For math or code, that can be a simple right-or-wrong check.

3

Compare to the group

Measure each answer against the group's average. Above average is good, below average is bad.

4

Nudge the model

Make above-average answers a little more likely and below-average ones less, in small steps.

Then repeat on the next prompt. No second network is ever trained. The group is the only yardstick.
