Key takeaways

Three things to carry forward

1

Thinking time is a new dial

Beyond making models bigger, you can make them think longer at answer time. More thinking buys more accuracy, and can stand in for a larger model.

2

The methods stack up

Chain-of-thought, then voting across many chains, then tree search, then guided search. Each rung adds one idea and spends a little more compute.

3

Right-or-wrong is enough

Rewarding only correct answers, checkable for maths and code, let models teach themselves to reason, backtrack, and self-check. That is what o1 and DeepSeek-R1 showed.
