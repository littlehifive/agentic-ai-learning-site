13.3 · 13.4 The landmark result

Reward only the right answer, and reasoning emerges on its own

The breakthrough: train the model with reinforcement learning where the reward is simply whether the final answer is correct, checkable automatically for maths and code. Given only that signal, models learned to write long chains of thought, to backtrack, and to check themselves, with no one teaching those habits directly.

2024

OpenAI o1, then o3

The commercial frontier. The model thinks in hidden reasoning tokens before replying, and more thinking budget reliably raises its score on hard problems. On a tough maths contest it jumps from low scores to high ones simply by being allowed to think.

2025

DeepSeek-R1

The first fully open reasoning model to rival o1. Trained with a rewards-for-correct-answers recipe and no step-by-step grader. Researchers watched an "aha moment": the model spontaneously began saying "Wait, let me reconsider" and fixing its own mistakes.

Think of it like coaching only by saying "right" or "wrong" at the end. Told nothing about technique, the learner invents good habits, checking work, trying another route, on their own, because those habits are what win.

In plain terms

Verifiable rewardA reward you can compute automatically, like running code against tests or checking a maths answer.
