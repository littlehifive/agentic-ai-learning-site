13.2 Test-time scaling methods

Each method adds one new capability

Step 1

Chain-of-thought

Adds explicit steps. The model writes out its working instead of jumping to an answer. One path, no recovery if it slips.

→one fragile path

Step 2

Self-consistency

Adds sampling and voting. Run many chains and take the majority answer, so scattered errors are outvoted by agreeing correct ones.

→no structure

Step 3

Tree-of-thoughts

Adds structured search. Branch into several next steps, judge them, and backtrack from dead ends instead of running blind chains.

→spends effort evenly

Step 4

Monte Carlo tree search

Adds learned guidance. Plays out branches, learns which pay off, and focuses compute on the most promising parts of the tree.

In plain terms

The patternEach rung keeps the one before it and adds a single idea: steps, then voting, then search, then guided search.
The costEach rung also spends more compute at answer time. You climb only as far as the problem and your budget require.
