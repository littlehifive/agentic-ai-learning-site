6.2 The implicit reward · the one knob

Beta sets how hard to push

DPO has essentially one dial, called beta. It governs the tension between two pulls: chase the preferred answers aggressively, or stay close to the reference model the training began from. A common starting value is 0.1.

Large beta

Push hard toward the preference

The model moves boldly to favour chosen answers. It can improve fast, but stray far from the reference and start forgetting skills it already had.

Small beta

Stay near the reference

The model edges toward the preference cautiously, keeping its existing behaviour mostly intact. Safer, but slower, and too small leaves almost no signal to learn from.

Think of it like a leash on a dog. Short leash, small beta: the model can only wander a little from where it started. Long leash, large beta: free to chase the preferred answer, with more risk of running off.

In plain terms

BetaThe single dial trading off boldness against staying close to the reference.
