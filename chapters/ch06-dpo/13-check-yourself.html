What does DPO remove that PPO needs?

Reveal ▸

The separate reward model and the whole reinforcement-learning loop. DPO keeps just two models, the policy and a frozen reference, and learns in one ordinary supervised step on preference pairs.

Where does the reward come from if there is no reward model?

Reveal ▸

From the model itself. The score is beta times the log of how likely the trained model finds an answer divided by how likely the frozen reference found it. The model is its own judge.

Review: Chapter 6

What does the beta knob control?

Reveal ▸

How hard to push toward the preferred answers versus staying close to the reference. Large beta moves boldly but risks forgetting; small beta stays safe but learns slowly. A common start is 0.1.
