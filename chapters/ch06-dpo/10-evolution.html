Where DPO sits in the lineage

Each step stripped out more machinery

2022

RLHF with PPO

The baseline. Train a reward model from preferences, then run a reinforcement loop with four models to chase its score. Powerful, but heavy and unstable.

→too much machinery

2023

DPO

Removes the loop. A clean solution lets the model judge itself, so alignment becomes one supervised step on preference pairs. Two models, no reward model.

→tune the trade-offs

After

DPO variants

Refine the recipe. Follow-ups handle noisy labels, drop even the reference model, or add reasoning quality. Same core idea, different dials.

In plain terms

RLHFReinforcement learning from human feedback; the family PPO belongs to.
The trendSame alignment goal, steadily fewer models and less moving machinery.
