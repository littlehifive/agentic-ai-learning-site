6.2 The objective

One loss does the whole job: widen the gap

With a score for each answer in hand, the goal collapses to a single instruction. Make the chosen answer's score higher than the rejected answer's score, by a comfortable margin. That is the entire training target.

train so that score(chosen) is comfortably above score(rejected)
Underneath, this is the same cross-entropy loss used in ordinary model training, just applied to a pair. The label is simply chosen should win. No rollouts, no value head, no live scoring loop.
Think of it like nudging two runners apart. Each step, push the preferred answer a little ahead and the other a little back, until there is a clear, settled gap between them.
