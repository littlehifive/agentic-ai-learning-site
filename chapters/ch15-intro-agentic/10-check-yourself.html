What is the one thing that turns a chatbot into an agent?

Reveal ▸

The loop. A chatbot answers once and stops. An agent observes, reasons, acts, looks at the result, and repeats, carrying state and changing real systems, until the goal is met or it asks a human.

Why not just write a better prompt instead of building all these layers?

Reveal ▸

A single model call cannot remember across sessions, reach outside knowledge, take safe actions, coordinate specialists, or stop itself when unsure. Each of those five needs is a layer, not a phrase.

Review: Chapter 15

When should you reach for a full autonomous agent?

Reveal ▸

Only when the task genuinely needs the model to choose its own steps. Prefer a single call or a fixed workflow first, since they are easier to test and trust, and add autonomy only where it earns its cost.
