What a single model call cannot do

Five challenges every agent must answer

The moment a model has to act over time instead of replying once, five problems appear. Each one is real, and each one maps directly to a layer of the stack. Use them as a design checklist.

✓ PersistenceRemember what it has done, what failed, and the context, across turns, sessions, and days. Maps to Memory.
✓ GroundingReach up-to-date, domain-specific knowledge that was never in its training data. Maps to RAG.
✓ ActionInteract with real systems, such as databases, APIs, files, and browsers, through clear interfaces. Maps to Tools and MCP.
✓ CoordinationSplit work that one agent cannot handle across specialists that delegate and collaborate. Maps to Multi-Agent and A2A.
✓ SafetyAdd guardrails, human oversight, and graceful fallback for when the agent is uncertain. Maps to the Harness.

In plain terms

Design checklistFor any agent, ask each in turn: does it need memory, knowledge, actions, other agents, and where do humans approve?
Graceful degradationWhen unsure, the agent slows down, asks, or stops, rather than guessing and acting anyway.
