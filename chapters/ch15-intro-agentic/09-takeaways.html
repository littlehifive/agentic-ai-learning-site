Key takeaways

Three things to carry forward

1

The loop is the agent

What makes a system agentic is the perceive, reason, act, observe loop with iteration. Anything that answers once and stops is a chatbot, not an agent.

2

Five challenges, five layers

Persistence, grounding, action, coordination, and safety are what one model call cannot do. Each maps to a layer of the stack, and to a chapter ahead.

3

Prefer the simplest design

Autonomy is a dial. Use a fixed workflow until a task truly needs the model to chart its own course, and engineer the system, not just the prompt.
