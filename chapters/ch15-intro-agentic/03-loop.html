The defining feature of an agent

One loop underneath: perceive, reason, act, observe

An agentic system receives observations from an environment, reasons about what to do next, takes an action, and feeds the result back in. It keeps going until the goal is reached or it stops to ask a person for input.

Step 1

Perceive

Take in observations: the user's message, a tool's output, fresh data from the world.

→

Step 2

Reason

Given the goal and what has happened so far, decide the single next step.

→

Step 3

Act

Do something in the world: call a tool, run code, query an API, send a request.

→

Step 4

Observe

Read the result of the action, then judge whether the goal is met.

Feed the result back as a new observation and repeat until the goal is met or a human is asked
Think of it like a person working through a checklist. You do a step, check the result, adjust the plan if something went wrong, and move to the next item. You stop when the list is done or you hit something only your manager can decide.
