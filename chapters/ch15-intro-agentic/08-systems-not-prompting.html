Maintaining the systems perspective

Reliable agents are engineered, not just prompted

It is tempting to think a clever prompt is all you need. In practice, the layers only work as one tightly integrated system, and the difference between a demo and a dependable agent is the engineering around the loop.

A prompt alone leaves you with

  • A loop that can run away with no clear point to stop
  • An agent that forgets what it already tried and repeats failures
  • Actions taken with no record of what happened or why
  • No safe path when the model is unsure or simply wrong

Engineering the system adds

  • Context management that keeps the loop within its memory budget
  • Error handling so a failed step is caught and retried, not ignored
  • Guardrails and human approval before costly or irreversible actions
  • Observability, so you can see what the agent did and debug it
Think of it like the gap between a recipe and a working kitchen. The recipe is the prompt. The kitchen is the timing, the safety checks, the cleanup, and the record-keeping that let you cook the same dish reliably, every night, for real customers.
