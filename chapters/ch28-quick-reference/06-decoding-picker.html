28.3 · Decoding methods, how the model picks each word

Choosing how the model writes

Same model, very different output. Pick by how much randomness and variety you want.

MethodTypical settingWhen to reach for it
Greedyno knobAlways take the single most likely word. Fast and repeatable, but flat and prone to loops.
TemperatureT = 0.7The main creativity dial. Lower is safer and more focused; higher is more varied and surprising.
Top-p (nucleus)p = 0.9 to 0.95Keep only the smallest set of words that together cover most of the probability, then sample. The common default.
Top-kk = 40 to 100Keep just the k most likely words and sample among them. A simpler cap on the pool.
Beam searchB = 4 to 8Track several whole candidate sentences at once and keep the best. Good for short exact outputs.
Repetition penaltyθ = 1.1 to 1.3Nudge down words already used, to stop the model from repeating itself.

In plain terms

TemperatureA dial on randomness. At zero the model is deterministic; higher values let it take more chances.
SamplingRolling weighted dice over the allowed words instead of always taking the top one.
