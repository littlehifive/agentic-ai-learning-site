Why grade the model only on its response, not the prompt?

Reveal ▸

We want it to learn how to answer, not to rewrite the question back. Ignoring the prompt tokens focuses the correction on response quality and stops the model from just memorising prompts. It is like grading a worksheet only on the answers.

Why can a small clean dataset beat a huge messy one?

Reveal ▸

The model copies whatever it is shown, flaws and all. A few thousand excellent, diverse examples teach a higher standard than a million sloppy ones. Quality over quantity, the same way a dozen great worked examples teach an apprentice more than a warehouse of rushed work.

How do you tell forgetting apart from the alignment tax?

Reveal ▸

Try to recover the skill. If careful prompting or a few examples bring it back, the knowledge was only suppressed, so it is the alignment tax. If nothing recovers it, the knowledge was overwritten, which is catastrophic forgetting and needs retraining.
