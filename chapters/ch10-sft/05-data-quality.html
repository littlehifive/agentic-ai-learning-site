10.6 SFT data quality

A small set of excellent examples beats a huge noisy one

It is tempting to throw as much data as possible at the model. In practice the opposite often wins. A few thousand carefully written, diverse examples can teach better behaviour than a million sloppy ones, because the model copies whatever it is shown, flaws and all. Quality over quantity.

Think of it like teaching an apprentice. A dozen beautifully crafted pieces to copy teach more than a warehouse of rushed, inconsistent work. The apprentice imitates the standard you set, so set a high one.

In plain terms

Quality over quantityFewer, cleaner, more varied examples usually outperform a larger pile of mediocre ones.
CoverageThe data should span the tasks you actually care about; a task missing here is hard to add later.
Garbage in, garbage outThe model imitates its examples, so errors and bad style in the data show up in the answers.
