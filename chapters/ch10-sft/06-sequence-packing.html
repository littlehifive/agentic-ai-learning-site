10.1 Sequence packing for efficiency

Pack short examples together so no compute is wasted

The model trains in fixed-width batches. Short examples normally get padded with filler to fill the width, and that filler can burn 50 to 80 percent of the work. Packing instead lines up several short examples back to back in one slot, with a divider so they do not bleed into each other.

Think of it like a moving truck. Shipping one small box per trip wastes the truck; packing several boxes into each load fills the same space and finishes in far fewer trips.

In plain terms

PaddingFiller tokens added to make every example the same width; the model does no useful learning on them.
Packing payoffTwo to four times faster on datasets with mixed lengths, with the same result.
