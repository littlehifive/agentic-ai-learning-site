10.6 SFT training duration

Two or three passes is usually enough

One full pass over the data is called an epoch. For instruction fine-tuning, two to three epochs is the sweet spot. Train far longer and the model stops learning the pattern and starts memorising the exact examples, which makes it brittle on anything new.

1

Too few

The lesson may not stick; the model has only skimmed the examples once.

2 to 3

Just right

Enough repetition to learn the behaviour while keeping answers varied and flexible.

10+

Too many

The model memorises the training set and gives stiff, repetitive answers on new prompts.

Think of it like studying for an exam. A couple of focused passes teach the ideas; cramming the same answer sheet fifty times means you recite it word for word but freeze on a question phrased differently.

In plain terms

EpochOne complete pass through the whole training dataset.
OverfittingWhen a model memorises its examples instead of learning the general pattern, so it does poorly on new inputs.
