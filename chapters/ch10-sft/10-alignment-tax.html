10.5 The alignment tax

Two reasons a tuned model can lose ability

When a model gets worse on a test after tuning, it is worth knowing why. There are two very different causes, and they call for different fixes. One destroys knowledge; the other just holds it back on purpose.

Catastrophic forgetting

An accident. The model's old knowledge is physically overwritten, so a skill is gone. The cure is to retrain or to protect the original weights while tuning.

Alignment tax

A deliberate trade-off. To keep answers safe and well-mannered, we rein in the model, so its raw ability dips. The knowledge is still there, just suppressed, and the leash can be loosened.

Think of it like a star athlete. Forgetting is an injury that takes a skill away; the alignment tax is a strict coach holding them back from risky moves. Ease the coaching and the skill returns; heal an injury and you must rebuild it.
