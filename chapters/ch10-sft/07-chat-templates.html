10.2 Chat templates and formatting

Special tokens tell the model who is speaking

A model only sees a flat stream of text, so we wrap each turn in agreed marker tokens that label it as the system, the user, or the assistant. This is the chat template. Use the wrong markers, or none, and the model gets confused about its role and answers noticeably worse.

<im_start> system You are a helpful assistant. <im_end> <im_start> user What is the capital of France? <im_end> <im_start> assistant The capital of France is Paris. <im_end>
Think of it like a script for a play. The character names in the margin tell each actor which lines are theirs. Strip out the names and the actors lose track of who says what.
