What you will gain

By the end, you will be able to make sense of

✓How LLMs work inside. Attention, position, and why design choices change what a model can do.
✓The systems beneath them. GPU memory, distributed training, and fast serving.
✓Efficient fine-tuning. LoRA, quantization, and distillation to adapt models cheaply.
✓Alignment with human preferences. RLHF, DPO, GRPO, and how to pick among them.
✓How reasoning models learn. How o1 and DeepSeek-R1 discover step-by-step thinking.
✓How to architect agents. Memory, tools, coordination, and rigorous evaluation.
The book assumes only basic neural networks and probability. Everything else is built from first principles, and this deck keeps it plain.
