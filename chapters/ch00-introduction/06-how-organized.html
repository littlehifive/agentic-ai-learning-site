How this guide is organized

Source: Roitman, Hitchhiker's Guide to Agentic AI, Fig. 1, p.33. Each stage maps to a part of this guide.

Part III
Reasoning
Teaching models to reason through multi-step problems.
Part IV
Evaluation
Measuring whether any of it actually works.
Part V
Agentic AI
Memory, tools, and orchestration for real agents.
Part VI
Assessment & Reference
Quizzes, a quick reference, and a look ahead.
