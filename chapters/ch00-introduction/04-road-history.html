A brief history

The road to agentic AI, in ten milestones

1997Deep Blue beats the world chess champion. Machines can win in narrow, well-defined games.
2011IBM Watson wins Jeopardy. AI can handle messy, open-domain questions at scale.
2012AlexNet wins ImageNet. Deep networks on GPUs learn from raw data, starting the deep-learning era.
2016AlphaGo beats the Go champion. Reinforcement learning masters a game too vast to brute-force.
2019-20GPT-2 / GPT-3. Scaling up transformers produces few-shot learning: new tasks from examples alone.
2020AlphaFold predicts protein structures, cracking a 50-year scientific problem.
2022ChatGPT and RLHF. Alignment turns a raw model into a genuinely useful assistant.
2023GPT-4. Multimodal input, longer context, and the first hints of tool use.
2024Reasoning models (o1, DeepSeek-R1). RL teaches step-by-step reasoning, not just answers.
2025Agentic AI. Reasoning, tools, memory, and orchestration converge into autonomous agents.
Every leap shares one arc: a new architecture, more scale, and a better learning signal.
