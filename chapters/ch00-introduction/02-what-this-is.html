About this deck

A simplified companion, not the book

What this is

An interactive, plain-English walk through the book's ideas, built for people who are new to the field. Each chapter becomes a short visual deck that explains the key terms and how the pieces fit together.

What this is not

It is not a replacement for the original. The math, the code, and the full detail live in the book. When you want depth, read Haggai Roitman's text directly.

All credit for the ideas, the structure, and the figures belongs to the author. This deck only reorganizes that material into a friendlier on-ramp. Book figures are reproduced for study, with their source and page shown on each.
