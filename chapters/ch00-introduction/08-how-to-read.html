How to read this deck

Three things to know before you start

Navigating

Pick a chapter from the home grid. Inside, use the arrow keys or the side arrows to move, click the dots to jump, and press Esc to return home.

Look for "In plain terms"

Most slides carry a gold-edged box that defines the technical words on that slide in one plain sentence each. No prior jargon needed.

Interactives

Some slides invite a click: step through a build, flip a question to its answer, or toggle two examples. Try them.

Every chapter follows the same shape: the core idea, then the key concepts explained one at a time, a worked example, common mistakes, and a short self-check. All ideas and figures are from the book by Haggai Roitman.
