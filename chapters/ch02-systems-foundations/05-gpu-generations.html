2.1.3 Common GPUs for LLM Training and Inference

Each new chip generation does more, faster

NVIDIA ships a new GPU family every couple of years. The names you hear, A100, H100, B200, are just successive generations. Each one adds more memory and faster math, so a model that once needed a room of chips can later fit on a handful.

2020 · Ampere

A100

Up to 80 GB of memory at 2 TB/s. The cost-effective workhorse for fine-tuning and budget training.

→3x faster training

2022 · Hopper

H100

Same 80 GB but 3.35 TB/s and far faster math (FP8). The standard chip for serious training today.

→2x over H100

2024 · Blackwell

B200

192 GB at 8 TB/s and the fastest links yet. Roughly twice an H100 for the largest models.

Think of it like car engines getting bigger and more efficient each model year: more horsepower (math speed) and a larger fuel tank (memory), so the same trip takes less time and fewer cars.

In plain terms

HBMThe GPU's main memory, stacked right on the chip; more of it means bigger models fit.
Bandwidth (TB/s)How fast that memory can be read; the real limit on how quickly a model runs.
