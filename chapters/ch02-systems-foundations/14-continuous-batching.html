2.2.4 Continuous Batching

Refill empty seats every single step

GPUs are most efficient handling many requests at once, in a batch. But replies finish at different times. The old way made everyone wait for the slowest reply before starting the next group, so the GPU idled. Continuous batching swaps finished replies for waiting ones after every step.

Old way

Wait for the whole batch

Start a group together, finish together. If one reply runs long and another is short, the GPU sits idle on the short one for hundreds of steps.

Result: lots of idle time

Continuous batching

Swap in new work each step

After every step, drop the replies that just finished, free their pages, and pull in waiting requests to fill the gaps. The batch never goes slack.

Result: GPU near 100% busy, 1.5 to 3x more throughput

Think of it like a ride that refills each empty seat the moment someone gets off, instead of waiting for every seat to clear before the next group boards. The ride is always full and always moving.

In plain terms

BatchA group of requests the GPU handles together for efficiency.
Continuous batchingRefreshing that group every step so no seat stays empty; it relies on PagedAttention to free and reuse memory instantly.
