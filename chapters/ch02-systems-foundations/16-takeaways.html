Key takeaways

Three things to carry forward

1

Memory speed is the limit

Chips do math far faster than they fetch data. Knowing whether a job is memory-bound or compute-bound tells you what to fix.

2

Match the split to the link

Big models span many GPUs. Chatty splits stay inside one machine on NVLink; quieter ones can cross the network.

3

Smart memory means cheap serving

Paging the KV cache and refilling the batch every step keep the GPU full, raising answers per dollar several times over.
