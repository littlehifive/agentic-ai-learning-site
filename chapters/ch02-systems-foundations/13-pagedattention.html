Two replies, A (navy) and B (gold), take pages wherever there is room. A lookup table remembers the order. Free pages are reused the instant a reply finishes.

PagedAttention borrows a classic operating-system trick. Instead of one big reserved block per reply, the notes are stored in many small, fixed-size pages, handed out only when a reply actually needs them. The pages can sit anywhere in memory; a small lookup table keeps track of the order. Waste drops to almost nothing, and three bonuses fall out for free.

Think of it like a library that lends books one at a time instead of reserving a whole shelf per reader. Far more readers fit, and a returned book is instantly available to the next person.
