2.2.7 vLLM: End-to-End System

Put together, the gains are dramatic

Paged memory, prefix sharing, and continuous batching all reinforce each other inside vLLM. On the same hardware running the same 70B model, the difference over a plain setup is not a few percent, it is several times over.

Throughput

8x

Up to 4,000 words per second, against roughly 500 the plain way.

Memory used well

90%+

Almost no waste, against 50 to 60% usable with fixed blocks.

Users at once

10x

Hundreds of concurrent replies, against 16 to 32 the plain way.

Think of it like the same building serving far more people, just by seating them efficiently rather than blocking off whole floors per guest. Same hardware, a fraction of the cost per answer.
