23.1 Core requirements for A2A communication

For agents to work together, five things must hold true

Any protocol that lets agents collaborate has to solve the same short list of problems. A2A is built directly around these five.

One

Discoverability

Agents can find other agents and learn what each one can do.

Two

Interoperability

Agents from different teams or vendors speak one common protocol.

Three

Asynchrony

Long tasks do not block the caller; results arrive later by callback or polling.

Four

Security

Agents prove who they are and enforce what each is allowed to ask.

Five

Observability

Every exchange is traceable for debugging and auditing.

Think of it like hiring a freelancer online: you find them, you both speak the same platform, you do not wait on hold while they work, you verify their identity, and there is a record of the whole job.

In plain terms

InteroperabilityDifferent systems agreeing on one way to talk so they just work together.
AsynchronySending a request and getting on with other work until the answer comes back.
