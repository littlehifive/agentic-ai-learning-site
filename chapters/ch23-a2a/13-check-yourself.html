What is an Agent Card and why does it matter?

Reveal ▸

It is an agent's public profile, hosted at a known address, listing its skills, how to authenticate, and where to send work. It lets other agents discover and call it with no hardcoded wiring, like a business card or a LinkedIn profile.

Why does A2A model work as a task with states?

Reveal ▸

Because jobs can take seconds or hours. A task with states like submitted, working, and completed lets the caller track progress, answer a clarifying question, and stream or get notified about results without holding a connection open the whole time.

How is A2A different from MCP?

Reveal ▸

MCP connects an agent downward to tools and data, where only the agent reasons. A2A connects an agent sideways to another agent, where both sides reason. They complement each other: an orchestrator delegates via A2A, and each specialist reaches its tools via MCP.
