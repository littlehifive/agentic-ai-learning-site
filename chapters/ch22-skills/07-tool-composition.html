22.2.3 Hierarchical skill composition

Combine small skills into one bigger capability

Skills can depend on other skills. A high-level skill calls a handful of simpler ones, and the orchestrator works out the order before anything runs. Sub-skills can be shared, so you build each piece once.

Deploy Application

One skill that calls the three smaller skills in the right order to ship a release.

Think of it like a recipe that calls smaller recipes. "Make lasagna" pulls in "make the sauce" and "make the pasta", each of which you can also use on its own for other dishes.

In plain terms

Sub-skillA smaller skill that a larger one depends on and calls.
OrchestratorThe part that resolves which sub-skills to run, and in what order.
